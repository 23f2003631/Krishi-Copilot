# Day-1 Demo Script

## Scenario

Wheat growers in Kanpur Nagar, Uttar Pradesh are near flowering stage. Humidity and light rainfall increase disease-risk messaging priority. Retailer stock for Tilt 250 EC is healthy, so the system recommends Hindi WhatsApp plus field-rep follow-up.

## Click Path

1. Open `/planner`.
2. Review crop, district, language, device, stock, weather, and historical engagement context.
3. Click **Generate Recommendations**.
4. Open the top recommendation and review why now, who to target, channel mix, and stock guardrail.
5. Click **Generate Content**.
6. Review Hindi WhatsApp, SMS, IVR, rep script, and visual concept previews.
7. Click **Send to Field Plan**.
8. Review field actions, retailer stock notes, expected leads, and export options.

## Talk Track

This is not a farmer app. It is a Syngenta internal control room that combines crop stage, weather risk, language, device, past engagement, retailer inventory, and field execution into one campaign decision loop.
